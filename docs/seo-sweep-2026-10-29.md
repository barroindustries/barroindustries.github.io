# SEO sweep — 2026-10-29

A full pass over barroindustries.com's search presence: a live crawl of all 32 sitemap URLs, Search Console,
the Google Business Profile, Bing, and a look at who ranks for the searches that matter. What Claude fixed is
in the commit; **what only the owner can do is the checklist at the bottom.**

## Where things stand

- **Search Console (last 28 days, 20–27 Oct data):** 21 clicks · 159 impressions · CTR 13.2% · avg position 18.2.
  Top pages: `/` (18 clicks / 79 impressions), `/areas-we-serve/` (2 / 27), `/services/lpg-gas-line-installation/` (1 / 19),
  `/work/` (1 / 16), `/services/` (0 / 21), work tables (0 / 12), fire suppression (0 / 11), fabrication (0 / 10).
  Many of the impressions are foreign searches (e.g. "kitchen hood fire suppression systems lakeland fl"); the site is
  one week old for Google, so this is a baseline, not a verdict.
- **Indexing:** 24 indexed, 2 not (the project-brief page, blocked by robots on purpose, and the `?quote=1` redirect, also intended).
  The 10-28 product pages checked (dish tables, wok ranges, griddles) are already indexed.
- **Sitemap resubmitted 2026-10-29:** Google now reads all 32 URLs (it had 25).
- **Technical health (live crawl):** all 32 URLs 200, self-canonical, no duplicate titles or descriptions, no broken internal
  links, no orphan pages, every image 200, all JSON-LD valid, http → https and no-slash → slash redirects correct.
- **Who ranks:** for the commercial searches ("commercial kitchen exhaust hood philippines", "stainless steel fabrication
  quezon city", "kitchen fire suppression system philippines", "commercial gas stove philippines") the results are directories,
  job boards, residential retailers and foreign suppliers. Philippine fabricators with real how-to content are rare, so
  well-made guides can win. Competitors seen: Merit Stainless, HBS Marketing (Balintawak), Fabtech, Gomeco, EBC Fabricators,
  MJD Industries, Chuan Hing — none showed reviews or buyer guides, so reviews and guides are where Barro can pull ahead.
- **"barro kitchens"** shares results with unrelated "barro" ceramics brands; reviews, the Business Profile and consistent
  listings (below) are what build the brand signal.

## Fixed in this sweep

1. **Opening hours in the structured data** — Monday to Saturday 07:00–17:00 (Sunday closed, the owner's ruling and the
   Business Profile's hours) on the homepage and the 25 content/product pages, and in words in the homepage's contact list.
2. **A branded "page not found"** (`/404.html`, noindex): GitHub Pages' plain "404 · GitHub Pages" page is replaced by one
   with the site header, footer and links to products, services, work, guides and contact.
3. **IndexNow**: after every deploy that publishes, the Pages deploy check pings Bing / Yandex / Seznam / Naver with the
   public pages that changed (`scripts/indexnow.js`; key file `/56a23bfe1bfdd002453891cc782415f3.txt` — never delete it).
   Bing gets the pages within minutes without a Webmaster Tools account. Google does not use IndexNow.
4. **Sitemap dates** — `scripts/sitemap-lastmod.js` sets every `<lastmod>` to the date its page last changed
   (`--check` reports stale ones); dates had been stuck at 10-22 on pages edited since.
5. **Internal links** to the least-linked product pages: the sink page's related cards now include dish tables, the stove
   page's include griddles / grillers / fryers and mention gas deck ovens.
6. **Logo** `icons/barro-kitchens.png` re-encoded losslessly (848 → 675 KB, identical pixels) — the app preloads it.
7. **/catalogue/ first paint** and **its product list markup** — see the commit (inlined CSS like every other public page;
   the ItemList entries point to the product pages).
8. **Three new guides** (see the commit / STATUS for whether they are live): commercial kitchen exhaust hoods, LPG gas lines
   for restaurants, and cleaning and caring for stainless steel.

## Left as is (deliberately)

- **Titles over 60 characters** (17 pages): Google truncates by width, not count, and the homepage title set on 10-28 is
  already showing. Shortening is a small gain and re-churns pages that are still settling — revisit after a month of data.
- **Two fresh-air pages** (`/services/exhaust-and-fresh-air-systems/` and `/services/fresh-air-systems/`): a deliberate split
  by search intent (exhaust hood vs "what is a fresh air system"); they link to each other.
- **The project-brief link** on every page goes to a robots-blocked page: intended (it is a form, not content).
- **FAQ rich results**: Google now shows them only for government and health sites. The markup is valid and harmless.
