# Configurator preview photos — what to prepare

The catalogue's configurators show a preview that changes with every option a customer picks.
Until a photo exists, a placeholder shows instead. You don't need every combination: when an
exact photo is missing, the site falls back to the closest one (e.g. "2 sinks" → "sink"), so
start with **Priority 1** and add more over time.

## How to shoot (applies to every photo)

- **Landscape 4:3**, at least **1600 × 1200 px**.
- **Plain white or light-grey background**, product fully in frame, a little space around it.
- **Same angle for every photo of one product**: front-left three-quarter view, camera at
  about counter height. Matching angles make the preview feel like one product changing.
- Even, soft light. No people, plate numbers, documents or other brands in shot.
- Real Barro-built units are best; a clean render is fine too.
- Send them as JPG or PNG. Name each file with the **File name** below. I convert them to web
  format and add them.

Total: **Priority 1 = 17 photos** (the most-used variants of each product);
**Priority 2 = 43 more** for full coverage.
