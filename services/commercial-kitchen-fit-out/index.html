The fit-out

What a fit-out includes

Everything the kitchen needs, planned together and built by one team.

  • Layout and drawings

    We assess your space, your operational flow and your requirements, then plan the layout and prepare drawings for your kitchen.

  • Cooking lines

    Stoves built to your layout, with a griller, griddle, oven or fryer added where you need one, plus fryers, griddles, grillers and ovens from the cooking catalogue.

  • Preparation and washing stations

    Stainless-steel preparation tables and sink tables built to your sizes, with the shelves and faucets you choose. See preparation and washing.

  • Baking equipment

    Insulated SS304 dough proofing cabinets and gas deck ovens.

  • Storage and refrigeration

    Stainless-steel cabinets, wall shelving and racks, with chillers and freezers for cold storage. See storage and refrigeration.

  • Exhaust hoods and ducting

    Stainless-steel range hoods and the ducting that carries the air out. Read about exhaust and fresh-air systems.

  • Fresh-air systems

    Fresh air supplied to replace what the exhaust removes, distributed through room diffusers.

  • Gas lines

    A centralised gas supply from your tank storage to the cooking line. Read about LPG gas line installation.

  • Fire suppression

    A wet chemical system integrated with the range hood. Read about kitchen fire suppression.

  • Delivery, installation and commissioning

    Your finished equipment is delivered to your site, installed there and commissioned for use.

Our process

How we work

One team carries your project from the first consultation through to after-sales support.

  1. 1

    Consultation & site visit

    We listen to your requirements and visit your site to take measurements.

  2. 2

    Design & layout

    We plan the layout and prepare drawings for your kitchen.

  3. 3

    Fabrication

    Built to order in SS304 or SS316, finished brushed, mirror or satin, with seams welded and ground smooth.

  4. 4

    Delivery

    Your finished equipment is delivered to your site.

  5. 5

    Installation & commissioning

    We install everything on site and commission it for use.

    After-sales support

    Service and support continue after handover.
