Kitchen exhaust and fresh-air systems

A well-ventilated kitchen is essential for both safety and comfort. We design, fabricate and install exhaust hoods, ducting and fresh-air systems that remove smoke, grease and heat while keeping the air in your kitchen moving.

We plan them together with your cooking line, gas line and fire suppression, as part of one kitchen layout.

How it works

Two paths, one balanced kitchen

A commercial kitchen ventilation system has two paths. A heavy-duty exhaust line removes heat, smoke and grease from over the cooking line. A fresh-air supply line replaces the air being pulled out, so the kitchen does not turn into a vacuum and the air circulates across its different zones through room diffusers.

Because the two paths depend on each other, we plan the exhaust and the fresh-air supply as one system. See also kitchen fire suppression, which is integrated with the range hood, and LPG gas line installation for the cooking line below it.

The system

What’s included

The hood, the ducting, the blowers and the diffusers, planned for your site and installed by one team.

  • Stainless-steel range hoods

    SS304 hoods over your cooking line, fitted with filters, lighting and integrated switch controls.

  • Exhaust ducting and blower

    Fully welded rectangular exhaust ducting powered by a centrifugal blower, with foil insulation and an access door for maintenance.

  • Fresh-air ducting and blower

    Fresh-air ducting with its own blower, integrated into the hood and distributed through room diffusers.

  • Room diffusers

    Square louvered diffusers that spread the fresh air across the zones of your kitchen.

  • Site assessment and layout

    We assess your site, customise the system to suit your kitchen and prepare drawings.

  • Installation and commissioning

    We deliver, install and commission the system on site.

A standard example

What one system can look like

A dual-path ventilation system

One standard example from our 2026 kitchen catalogue. Every system is custom-fabricated to meet the requirements of your site.

Hood
A 4-metre SS304 stainless-steel range hood with filters, lighting and integrated switch controls.
Exhaust
A 10-metre rectangular duct of fully welded BI sheet, powered by a 3HP centrifugal blower, with foil insulation and an access door for maintenance.
Fresh air
A 15-metre GI (galvanised iron) duct system with a 3HP blower, integrated into the hood and distributed through about 8 room diffusers.

These dimensions and specifications are standard examples and are not exhaustive. All equipment can be custom-fabricated to meet your specific site requirements.
