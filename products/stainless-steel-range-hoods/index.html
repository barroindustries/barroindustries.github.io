Stainless steel range hoods, wall, island or slim

Stainless steel kitchen exhaust hoods for restaurant, hotel, hospital, school and commissary kitchens in the Philippines. Choose a wall, island or slim canopy, baffle grease filters and lighting, then set your own length and depth.

Each range hood is built to order in food-grade SS304 stainless steel as standard, or laboratory-grade SS316 on request, in a brushed, mirror or satin finish, with seams welded and ground smooth.

Choose your range hood

A range hood is the canopy over your cooking line. Choose its type, its filters and its lighting, then give us your length and depth.

What you can choose

Every combination is made to order.

Type
A wall canopy, against a wall and over a cooking line; an island canopy, open on all four sides, over a cooking block that stands on its own; or a slim, low-profile canopy for a low ceiling or a counter line.
Grease filters
Stainless baffle filters, or none (an open canopy) where the filters are fitted elsewhere in the duct.
Lighting
With a lamp and switch, or without.
Size
Made to your length and depth. A wall canopy is at least 500 mm deep, an island canopy at least 1200 mm long and 700 mm deep, and a slim canopy no deeper than 500 mm. The canopy height follows the type: about 500 mm for a wall or island canopy and about 350 mm for a slim one.
Construction
SS304 stainless steel · welded canopy with grease trough and oil cup · wall-mounted canopy · stainless baffle grease filters.
Material
Food-grade SS304 as standard, or laboratory-grade SS316 on request.
Finish
Brushed, mirror or satin.

Questions and answers

Stainless steel range hood questions

How much is a stainless steel range hood?

Prices depend on size and options. Get an estimated range online in minutes — no account needed. Choose the type of canopy, the filters, the lighting and the size in the online configurator and add the other items you need, and the quote request shows an estimated range. We then review your request and send you the final quotation.

What types of range hood can I choose?

A wall canopy for a cooking line against a wall, an island canopy that is open on all four sides, or a slim, low-profile canopy for a low ceiling or a counter line. You set the length and the depth.

Does a range hood come with grease filters and lighting?

Yes, as standard: stainless baffle grease filters and a lamp with a switch. You can choose an open canopy with no filters, where they are fitted elsewhere in the duct, and you can leave the lighting out.

Which stainless steel do you use for range hoods?

Food-grade SS304 stainless steel as standard, and laboratory-grade SS316 on request, in a brushed, mirror or satin finish.

Do you install your range hoods, and is there a warranty?

Yes. We design, fabricate, deliver and install, then commission everything on site. Service and support continue after handover. We give a 6-month service warranty on our equipment and work. Full-kitchen projects also include 12 months of preventive maintenance service (PMS) with KitchenShield™.
