Areas we serve across Luzon

Our project locations

13 locations, three regions

We have completed projects in each of these places.

North Luzon

  • Ilocos Sur
  • La Union
  • Baguio
  • La Trinidad

We have completed projects in four northern locations, from the coast of Ilocos Sur and La Union to Baguio and La Trinidad in the Cordillera highlands. In La Union, we delivered and installed stainless-steel equipment on the campus of Don Mariano Marcos Memorial State University (DMMMSU).

Central Luzon

  • Tarlac
  • Pampanga
  • Bulacan
  • Iba, Zambales
  • Subic

Our Central Luzon projects span the central plain, in Tarlac, Pampanga and Bulacan, and the Zambales coast, in Iba and Subic.

Metro Manila & South

  • Manila
  • Cavite
  • Laguna
  • Batangas

We have completed projects in Manila and in the provinces south of it, from Cavite and Laguna down to Batangas. Our office is in Tandang Sora, Quezon City.
