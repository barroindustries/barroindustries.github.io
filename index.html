What we do

Services

The same high-quality build runs through every service: seams welded and ground smooth, surfaces polished to a professional finish, and every piece built to order.

01

Commercial & commissary kitchen fit-outs

Design-build from concept to completion: cooking lines, preparation and washing stations, baking equipment, exhaust hoods and ducting, fresh-air systems, gas lines and fire suppression. One contractor, from drawings to commissioning.

Our process

How we work

One team carries your project from the first consultation through to after-sales support.

  1. 1

    Consultation & site visit

    We listen to your requirements and visit your site to take measurements.

  2. 2

    Design & layout

    We plan the layout and prepare drawings for your kitchen.

  3. 3

    Fabrication

    Built to order in food-grade SS304, with seams welded and ground smooth.

  4. 4

    Delivery

    Your finished equipment is delivered to your site.

  5. 5

    Installation & commissioning

    We install everything on site and commission it for use.

  6. 6

    After-sales support
