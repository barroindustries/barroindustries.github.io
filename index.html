Our process

How we work

One team carries your project from the first consultation through to after-sales support.

  1. 1

    Consultation & site visit

    We listen to your requirements and visit your site to take measurements.

  2. 2

    Design & layout

    We plan the layout and prepare drawings for your kitchen.

  3. 3

    Fabrication

    Built to order in SS304 or SS316, finished brushed, mirror or satin, with seams welded and ground smooth.

  4. 4

    Delivery

    Your finished equipment is delivered to your site.

  5. 5

    Installation & commissioning

    We install everything on site and commission it for use.

  6. 6

    After-sales support

    Service and support continue after handover.
