❓ Quote Builder — Help

How a quote gets made

  1. Pick the company — Barro Kitchens / Barro Industries / Brilliant Steel. Letterhead, quote number and payment details follow it.
  2. Quote number (Client Data) — Auto builds the official number; Manual is only for re-issuing.
  3. Client information (Client Data) — type the name; existing clients autocomplete.
  4. Add items (Estimator) — filter by category or search; a calculator opens per product (dimensions, unit, qty). ➕ Custom item for non-catalog work — the 🧮 BOM & computation popup on its line takes the bill of materials and labor days, and the item prices itself. ＋ Section for Option A / Option B quotes.
  5. Pricing is automatic (Estimator) — direct cost = materials + 6% waste + 80% labor (real crew-days override it) + 10% consumables, and every costed line sells at direct × 2.50 — of each selling peso, 40¢ is direct cost, 30¢ overhead, 30¢ markup — rounded to the nearest ₱100 so prices end in two zeros. Type a price to override — it sticks, and the cost panel shows the markup your price really gives. VAT 12%, discount (percent or ₱), agent commission and Options mode sit below the items.
  6. Miscellaneous costs (Estimator) — delivery, permits, subcontractors go in the misc table. No markup on pass-through costs: each quotes at cost + 25% OH, rounded to ₱100. Tick On quote? to show it as its own line, or untick and → distribute it into the item prices you choose.
  7. Details (Client Data) — delivery & installation, timeline, payment schedule, remarks, terms.
  8. Verify & File — completeness checklist; filing makes the quote official.
  9. Print / Share — Print/PDF for the client, Agent Copy (confidential, with commission), Share link (filed quotes only).

The three tabs

Client Data — the document form: everything that prints on the quotation, with a 👁 Preview button that shows the finished document exactly as the customer sees it.

Estimator — the numbers: items, additional costs, and the calculator — the build waterfall, the OH% and MK% dials (together always 150% of direct), and a per-line experiment table with editable prices.

Terms — the terms & conditions, per quote, including the ±5mm measurement tolerance.

The Estimator may be hidden depending on your account.

Good to know

  • 📝 Your work auto-saves as a draft; an unfiled quote offers to resume next time.
  • 🧾 Agent Copy is confidential — never send it to the client.
  • 🔗 Share requires the quote to be filed first — it will prompt you.
  • 💯 Prices end in two zeros — each computed line rounds to the nearest ₱100 (₱85,500, ₱7,500); the markup quietly absorbs the difference. "↺ re-apply computed prices" in the cost panel clears any hand-typed overrides.
  • 📦 Product Database — opens right from the builder (the button beside the database status). Add or edit products and their capital costs, then ← Back to the quote.
