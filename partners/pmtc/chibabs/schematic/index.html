Diagram 3 · Process

How an inasal order moves through the kitchen

One diagram per process, drawn on the same plan. Steps 01–08 follow one order from the chiller to the diner; 09–12 are the support routines that run alongside.

01

Receiving supplies

  1. 1Supplies come in through the back door and the kitchen entry.
  2. 2Chicken and marinated meat go straight into the chillers.
  3. 3Rice, dry goods and charcoal go on the storage racks.
02

Take meat from the chiller

  1. 1Take marinated chicken from the chiller.
  2. 2Skewer or portion it on the prep sink table.
  3. 3Bring it to the griller.
03

Take charcoal from the rack

  1. 1Take charcoal from the box at the bottom of the storage rack.
  2. 2Load and light both grillers, with the range hood running.
04

Grill and place in hot holding

  1. 1Grill the chicken in batches ahead of the rush.
  2. 2Move cooked chicken into the hot holding cabinet, ready for orders.
05

Order is taken

  1. 1Customer orders and pays at the POS.
  2. 2The order is sent straight to an order printer or kitchen display (KDS) at the dispatch table. Nobody has to carry a slip.
  3. 3Dispatch reads the ticket and calls it to the griller. The ticket stays at dispatch to check the tray before it goes out.
06

Back to the griller

  1. 1Take the portion out of hot holding.
  2. 2Finish it on the griller: reheat and char so it's served hot and smoky.
07

Plating

  1. 1Chicken comes off the griller.
  2. 2Rice from the steamer (next to hot holding).
  3. 3Plate or bowl from the storage rack.
  4. 4Assemble on the dispatch table with heated utensils.
08

Dispatch and serve

  1. 1The plated order goes out through the pass-thru.
  2. 2At the service table, diners help themselves to unli soup, rice and condiments.
  3. 3The order is carried to diners through the hallway.
09

Unli soup and rice refill

  1. 1Soup stays hot in the stock pot.
  2. 2Rice comes from the steamer.
  3. 3Refill the service table's soup and rice pots through the pass-thru.
10

Dish return and washing

  1. 1Diners leave used dishes at the dish return.
  2. 2Dishes pass through the dish pass-thru onto the landing.
  3. 3Scrape solid and liquid waste into separate bins.
  4. 4Wash in the double sink.
  5. 5Air-dry on the clean racks.
  6. 6Clean plates go back to the storage rack by the entry.
11

Waste out

  1. 1Tie and seal the bags at the bins.
  2. 2Carry them along the aisle to the kitchen entry.
  3. 3Out through the back door, after service or between rushes.
12

Halo-halo and drinks

  1. 1Customer orders at the POS.
  2. 2Halo-halo is made at the station behind the counter.
  3. 3Bottled drinks come from the beverage fridge.
  4. 4Handed over to the customer at the counter.
Future R&D

Room to grow the menu

The layout can take new menu tests without moving anything. Frying uses the 4-burner range as quoted; baking needs one spec change to the same unit.

R&D 1

Fried menu on the 4-burner range

  1. 1Chicken, pork or fish comes from the chiller. Breaded items get coated at the prep table.
  2. 2Cooking oil is stored on the storage rack.
  3. 3Fry in a deep kawali or fry pot on one high-pressure burner, with the hood running. The other burners stay free for sauces and sides.
  4. 4Drain on a wire rack at the dispatch table, then plate.
  5. 5It goes out through the pass-thru like any other order.
Good for testingChicken skin, fried chicken, lumpia, tokwa, calamares: sides that pair with inasal and unli rice.
Before going liveOil thermometer, lid and fire blanket or K-class extinguisher by the range; splash guard on the grill side; used oil collected in sealed containers, never poured into the dish sink; grease trap on the sink drain.
If frying sellsReplace the kawali with a dedicated gas deep fryer. Plan it as a separate unit under the hood so the 4-burner stays free.
R&D 2

Oven-baked option under the 4-burner

  1. 1Swap the open undershelf on quote item #5 for a gas oven base. The footprint (850 × 650 × 850), gas drop and hood coverage stay the same.
  2. 2The burners on top work as quoted, and the oven runs on its own thermostat.
  3. 3Pans come out of the oven straight onto the dispatch table across the aisle.
Good for testingBaked or roasted chicken, baked rice dishes, leche flan and other baked desserts.
Watch the aisleThe oven door drops about 450 mm into a 1,030 mm aisle, leaving about 580 mm to pass. Keep the front row clear while the oven is open.
Quote impactPrice #5 as an option: "4-Burner High Pressure Range with Oven Base". Decide before fabrication, because retrofitting an oven into the undershelf later is costly.
Zone descriptions

What each zone does and what's in it

Orange item numbers mean the layout needs it but the quotation doesn't have it yet.

Zone 1 · Back of house

Storage — cold & dry

First stop for all deliveries. Chicken and marinated meat go into the chillers; rice, charcoal, bottled goods and dry supplies go on the racks. Keep it close to the kitchen entry so crates don't travel down the cook line.

Equipment
  • #182 × 4-Door Chiller/Freezer (1220 × 760) — stacked along the end wall, doors opening into a clear zone with a rack on either side
  • #73 × SS Storage Rack, 5 layers — two flanking the chiller door zone (back and front wall, charcoal box at the bottom), one after the entry for clean plates & bowls (quoted qty 1)
Next to

Preparation (must touch) · Cooking (chiller within one step of the grill)

Zone 2 · Back of house

Preparation

Washing, cutting, skewering and marinating before grilling. Table with chopping board and a sink for washing ingredients, sitting between the chiller and the grill.

Equipment
  • #2SS Sink Table lengthened to 1500 × 650 with chopping board and gooseneck faucet — beside the griller
  • #15SS Wall Cladding with shelves & faucets behind the line
  • #16SS Wall Shelves on available walls
Next to

Storage · Cooking. Keep veggies/condiment prep away from raw chicken on the same board.

Zone 3 · Back of house

Cooking

The hot line along the back wall. Grills handle inasal; the range handles sides; the stock pot runs the unli soup. One continuous hood runs over the grills, range and stock pot. The back-wall row (rack, prep table and cook line) stands about 120 mm off the wall so the gas pipe, entering through the end-wall corner straight from the tank farm, runs in the gap behind it.

Equipment
  • #12 × SS Charcoal Griller, 2 layers (1350 × 650)
  • #5SS 4-Burner High Pressure Range (850)
  • #6SS 3-Burner High Pressure Stock Pot (1200, low 475 H)
  • gasLPG line from the outside tank farm, run along the back wall behind the grillers, 4-burner and stock pot
  • #141 × SS Range Hood, ≈ 4750 long — over grills, range and stock pot
Next to

Preparation · Plating across the aisle. Keep away from the dish sink.

Zone 4 · Kitchen → counter

Plating & Dispatch

Where every order is assembled on one 2 m dispatch table: chicken from hot holding right beside it, rice from the steamer next to that, plates from the storage rack by the entry, heated utensils. Plated food leaves only through the pass-thru. On the other side, the 2 m service table holds the self-serve unli soup, unli rice and condiments, with storage underneath.

Equipment
  • #3SS Hot Holding Cabinet, electric, 15 layers
  • #10SS Rice Steamer, 12 layers — beside hot holding
  • #4SS Preparation Table 2000, 3 layers — 1 dispatch (inside), 1 service table (outside)
  • newOrder printer or kitchen display (KDS) at the dispatch table, linked to the POS
  • #19SS Utensils Heater, 4 cups
  • #8SS Pass-Thru Window 1500 (open)
  • newSoup/rice warmers and condiment trays for the service table
Next to

Cooking · Service table (through pass-thru, facing the hallway) · Clean dish racks

Zone 5 · Back of house

Dishwashing & Waste

Dirty plates come in through their own pass-thru, get scraped and segregated (solid food waste, liquid waste, trash), washed in the double sink, then dried on the clean racks nearest dispatch. Bagged waste goes out through the kitchen entry and the back door beside the comfort room — schedule it after service or between rushes, and keep bins sealed.

Equipment
  • #11SS Double-Tub Dishwashing Sink, lengthened to ≈ 2400 with drainboards — full right wall
  • #132 × SS Dish Rack, 4 layers (clean)
  • newSecond pass-thru window for dish return
  • newDirty-dish landing table + solid/liquid waste bins, trash bin
Next to

Dish return counter · Dispatch (for clean plates). Separated from the hot line.

Zone 6 · Front of house

POS, Halo-Halo & Beverage

Customers order and pay at the POS. Behind the cashier, the halo-halo station and the bottled-beverage fridge let one counter staff serve desserts and drinks without entering the kitchen.

Equipment
  • #17SS Halo-Halo Station, lengthened to 2400 (matches sink wall), with undercounter chiller/freezer, sink, ice bin, ice maker, cup storage
  • newPOS counter, 2400 long
  • newBeverage display fridge (or client-supplied)
Next to

Dining area to the right · Dish return via hallway
